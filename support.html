Quick answers

🔑 Can't join a room?

Double-check the four-letter room code and ask the host whether the game has started. New players can join while the room is in the lobby.

📱 Can't see nearby players?

Keep participating devices near one another. Check that Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are enabled and grant the app relevant local-network access if your device prompts you.

🎶 Unsure how to play?

Create or join a room, then take turns answering Bollywood music challenges. Sing your answer before the timer runs out!

🏆 Want to report a score issue?

Tell us what happened and which screen you were on. If you're the host, check the game's score-adjustment controls.

Reporting a bug? Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help. Please don't send passwords or sensitive personal information.
