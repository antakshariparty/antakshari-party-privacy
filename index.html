Antakshari Party — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026
Applies to: Antakshari Party, iOS Version 2.0, Build 9

Antakshari Party is a Bollywood singing party game that can be played solo or with nearby players. It does not require an account.

Information used by the app

When you create or join a game, you may enter a player or team name. The app uses that name together with a room code, a locally generated peer identifier, game settings, current challenges, and scores to run the game and keep nearby players’ screens in sync. The app stores the randomly generated peer identifier on your device so nearby multiplayer can identify that device between game sessions. This identifier is not an advertising identifier and is not used for tracking.

Based on the functionality of this release, the developer does not receive player names, room codes, scores, answers, song choices, or gameplay history on a developer-operated server. There are no user accounts or cloud-hosted game histories in this release.

Nearby multiplayer and local network access

Nearby multiplayer uses Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity technology, which can communicate over nearby network connections. To discover and connect to a room, iOS may ask you to allow Local Network access. While you are connected, the app exchanges the player or team names, peer identifiers, room code, scores, game settings, and current game state needed to play with other devices in that room. Other participants can see the player/team names and scores shown by the app. The app does not send this gameplay information to a developer-operated server.

You can play without joining a nearby game. You can also disconnect from a room or turn off the app’s Local Network permission in iOS Settings; nearby multiplayer will then be unavailable until access is restored.

Microphone, recordings, and device permissions

The game asks players to sing aloud in person. This release does not use the device microphone to record or upload singing, and it does not request access to your contacts, photos, or precise location for gameplay.

Ads, analytics, and purchases

Build 9 does not contain advertisements, advertising or analytics SDKs, tracking functionality, or in-app purchases. The developer does not sell player or gameplay data.

Retention and deletion

Game-room information is used during an active game session; the app does not maintain a developer-hosted record of your past games. The app stores its randomly generated peer identifier locally on your device. To stop participating in a game, leave the room or close the game. You can remove app-local information by deleting Antakshari Party from your device. Because this release does not provide cloud accounts or developer-hosted gameplay storage, there is no cloud game-history account to delete.

Children

The app is designed as a social music game and does not ask users to create accounts or intentionally collect children’s personal information on a developer-operated server. A player’s chosen name is visible to others who join the same nearby game room. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information as a player or team name.

Changes to this policy

If a future release adds online multiplayer, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, accounts, purchases, or other changes to data handling, this policy and the app’s App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate.
